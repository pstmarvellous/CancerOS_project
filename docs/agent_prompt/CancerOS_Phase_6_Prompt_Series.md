## Evaluation pools and frozen protocols

Keep engineering fixtures, curated real integration cases, genuine longitudinal demonstration, independent original-patient model evaluation and reviewed language challenges separate. Group every visit, processed-source alias, lesion crop, augmentation and synthetic descendant by original patient for splitting. A polished demo case is curated, not an unseen independent test. Unknown checkpoint cohort overlap prevents a clean independent-performance claim.

Freeze source/rights/patient/split/annotation/time/variable manifests, protocol, code/image/environment/config/model/prompt/corpus and tolerance settings before final scoring. Fit/tune on allowed development data only. If evaluation labels were inspected for tuning, disclose/relabel that use and obtain a fresh set when possible. Retain all attempted/eligible/excluded/failed/abstained/scored counts and reasons; no best-run selection or silent NaN failure exclusion.

Use independent calculations/tools for physical reference, not the production helper as its own oracle. Proposed SPEC-011 checks include volume max(1e-6 mm3, 1e-9 × expected), bounded coordinate round trip <=1e-5 mm and synthetic nonzero-target realization max(one voxel volume, 1% target), unless stricter predeclared policy applies. These are engineering checks, not clinical thresholds. Imported-mask self-overlap is not model accuracy.

Simulation regression targets five families × at least ten fixed seeds, with a repeated full five-family set, declared target/noise rules and actual geometry coverage. The language challenge target is sixty reviewed cases (twenty grounded, twenty missing/conflicting/temporal/negation, twenty adversarial/prohibited), ideally three model runs/case if live inference is enabled. No available specialist/model/real notes means honest partial evidence, not invented review or live performance.

Required frozen-suite interpretation gates remain 100% numeric/unit/origin correctness for published output, zero published critical prohibited claims and correct abstention for every predefined critical insufficient-evidence case. Citation resolution differs from meaningful support. Finite-suite success is not proof of zero deployed risk. Forecasting remains separately gated; a real pair demonstrates chronology but cannot establish generalization or calibration.

## Deployment, cost and recovery rules

Keep the established modular monolith plus bounded CPU workers, private object storage and PostgreSQL. Choose one supported topology using the actual account, source-policy region and authorized budget. Verify current platform behavior/prices during implementation; no assumed cloud credits or local GPU training. Separate environments/identities/data/queues/secrets and keep initial staging restricted while controls are completed.

Configuration/build validation is not a deployed application. A green health check is not release evidence. A backup schedule is not recoverability. A budget alert is not a hard cost cap. A signed URL may survive withdrawal until expiry unless tested revocation/proxy behavior says otherwise. Source immutability does not justify retention locks that violate approved deletion obligations.

Test SQL plus exact referenced object versions, key recovery and historical reconstruction in an isolated restore target. Set explicit RPO/RTO and measure achieved recovery/loss. Rollback uses pinned compatible application/config versions; destructive database downgrade or restoring over current writes is not a default rollback. Report actual residual risks and block affected promotion until resolved.

Use already authorized scope/budget rather than repeatedly requesting routine permission. If account/credentials/spending authority are absent, prepare concrete deployable artifacts and the exact remaining decision; do not silently incur costs or claim a cloud URL. This pack itself authorizes prompt drafting, not external submission or new unrestricted publishing.

## Demo integrity and usability

Five scenario entries cover SC-01–SC-05, not necessarily five distinct real patients. Report entries/branches/original patients separately and disclose reused baselines. Each branch references an observed baseline plus at least three simulated follow-ups. No synthetic result or MRI phase can replace the genuine two-exam case required for full authentic temporal demonstration.

Judge accounts are viewers. Source-backed live writes use an authorized presenter in an isolated project. Guide restart resets selection/camera/step progress only; administrative reseeding is separate and cannot erase source history. Prepared/saved/rule-based/static/recorded outputs keep accurate labels and exact source context. They do not become the result of a pending live job or close missing real/interactive gates.

Confirm current official event deadline/timezone, rubric, required integrations, format and duration during COS-098. Five minutes and ten focused days are planning assumptions, not verified organizer rules; finalist status is aspirational until established. Provide a readable sequence and a reviewable submission package; do not claim submission success without an explicitly authorized completed action.

Maintain the Phase 5 accessibility/failure requirements. Record real participants and device/browser/network; a screenshot is not an interactive or clinical usability test. A participant interpreting simulated values as real findings is a blocking disclosure defect. Measure actual metadata/manifest/mesh/camera performance against documented targets, including slow/failed trials.

## Final evidence and release disposition

Every report includes exact version/input identity, reference definition, unit of analysis, actual reviewer role, denominators, failures and limitations. Report small cohorts descriptively rather than manufacturing confidence from correlated visits/voxels/branches. Publish only authorized redacted evidence and keep restricted source maps/logs private.
